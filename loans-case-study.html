Problem Statement

The IIFL Loans app enabled customers to apply for loans, manage repayments, track loan status, and access documents without visiting a branch or calling support. But the experience was not matching the promise.

Most of our users were in tier 2, tier 3 cities, and villages across India, with Android contributing 86% of usage.

Even with meaningful traffic, the app was underperforming: it had a 3.6 rating on Google Play and 2.2 on the App Store. Customers kept calling out the same issues — unclear loan status, session timeouts on the home screen, outdated referral campaigns, and convenience fees during repayment.

In practice, this meant customers still relied on branches for basic servicing, which increased operational load for the bank.

Overview

Owned product improvements for the IIFL Loans app focused on simplifying repayments, improving servicing reliability, and rebuilding customer trust across tier 2 and tier 3 India.

  • App ratings improved from 2.2 → 4.4 (App Store) and 3.6 → 4.1 (Google Play)
  • Monthly active users grew from 90K → 166K
  • Generated 400+ weekly reviews and ratings
  • Reduced customer dependence on branches for servicing
  • Improved repayment adoption through UPI-led flows

Understanding the User Personas — the humans in loop

As a Product Manager in the Digital Strategy team, my role was to improve both the functionality and the credibility of the app. Because the app ranked well for IIFL-related searches, it shaped how customers viewed the brand — this was not just a product problem, but a trust problem.

I aligned closely with developers to understand the technical constraints, friction points, and what could realistically be improved first.

Alongside the customer experience, I also spent time understanding engineering constraints, technical debt, and what roadmap could re-energize the team internally.

I also looked at the customer side closely. The app had around 90K MAU and an average time spent of 2 minutes 10 seconds, which told us people were already coming in with intent.

For a loan customer, checking repayment status or due dates is not a daily activity — it is a high-stakes moment that needs to be simple and reliable.

Using console metrics and customer sentiment analysis, I mapped the most common journeys and the most painful issues.

That is where the work became very human for me: answering customer frustration clearly, acknowledging their concerns without defensiveness, and making sure they felt heard while we fixed the product in the next releases.

Solution-ing? The core experiences

The first priority was to fix the basics. We cleaned up instrumentation so we could trust the funnel data, then improved the home screen experience to make the app easier to understand.

That included:

  • bolding important loan details on cards
  • showing clearer error states when data did not load
  • adding more actionable guidance during new loan application flows

With support from design and content, we rolled out these updates as a bug-fix release.

Reducing the Operational Overhead — Repayments and loan documentation

The repayment flow became the most critical part of the servicing experience. It directly affected credit score, future eligibility, and customer confidence in the product.

If this journey is unclear, the app loses trust very quickly.

We redesigned the repayment experience so customers could immediately see:

  • when the loan was due
  • how much was payable
  • a clear “Pay Now” action
  • a static list of loans with repayment dates

We also introduced UPI-based payments and aligned the journey with NPCI’s no-convenience-fee expectation, which reduced friction significantly.

The release created visible momentum. Once customers saw a simpler servicing flow and received notification nudges, active users, impressions, and login conversions began climbing quickly.

Repayment Journey Transformation

Before

  • Customers struggled to identify active loans
  • Repayment visibility was unclear
  • Session timeouts disrupted servicing flows
  • Convenience fees increased repayment friction
  • Customers continued depending on branches

After

  • Clear loan visibility on dashboard
  • Repayment amounts surfaced immediately
  • UPI-based repayments simplified payments
  • Notification nudges improved repayment awareness
  • Customers completed servicing directly within the app

Placing our App on the Map

As adoption increased, app-store reputation became a key part of the product experience itself.

After a successful repayment, we introduced a rating prompt at the right moment in the journey — not too early, and not randomly.

That small change made a big difference.

Customers began leaving more action-oriented feedback, and the app’s visibility improved across finance app rankings as ratings rose on both App Store and Google Play.

Within 45 days of launch, the app reached 4.4 on the App Store and 4.1 on Google Play.

We also crossed 400+ weekly reviews, helping push the app beyond the 4-star mark on Google Play.

Monthly active users grew from 90K to 166K after the changes, alongside stronger repayment engagement and higher servicing activity within the app.

The app was now better positioned for loan top-ups, cross-sell opportunities, and real-time repayment communication.

More importantly, the team got renewed confidence in the product direction, and the work created visible recognition across functions.
