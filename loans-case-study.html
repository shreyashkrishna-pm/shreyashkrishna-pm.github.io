01 · Problem

The app users trusted with money had stopped feeling trustworthy

The Loans app helped customers apply for new loans, make timely repayments with real-time loan status, and access financial documents without local branch visits.

Most users were in tier 2, tier 3 cities, and villages across India, with Android contributing 86% of usage. Even with meaningful traffic, the app was underperforming — 2.2 / 5 on iOS, 3.6 / 5 on Android — and reviews highlighted three core problems:

  • unclear loan status caused by session timeouts and financial jargon
  • outdated referral campaigns and stale content
  • repayment friction from convenience fees

In practice, customers still relied on branches for basic servicing — which in turn increased operational load for branch officers.

TL;DR

Owned product improvements for the Loans app focused on simplifying repayments, improving servicing reliability, and rebuilding customer trust across tier 2 / 3 India.

  • Ratings 2.2 → 4.4 (iOS), 3.6 → 4.1 (Android)
  • MAU grew 90K → 166K
  • 400+ weekly reviews
  • Reduced branch dependence for servicing
  • UPI-led repayment adoption

02 · Discovery

Understanding the core experience

As a Product Manager in the Digital Strategy team, my role was to improve both the functionality and the credibility of the app. Because the app ranked high for gold and personal loan searches, it shaped how customers viewed the brand — making this a trust problem.

To find where friction lived, I worked closely with engineering across Android and iOS to evaluate:

  • instrumentation gaps on iOS and Android
  • session timeout behavior
  • repayment journey drop-offs
  • failure states across critical flows

The app had ~90K MAU with an average session of 2m 10s. That told us users arrived with strong intent.

Customers continued relying on branches for even basic servicing needs.

The human element

That's where the work became deeply human: responding promptly to customer frustration on the Play Store and App Store, acknowledging concerns without being defensive, and ensuring users felt heard while we addressed issues in subsequent releases.

03 · Stabilize

Fixing the basics first

We cleaned up instrumentation of all user journeys on the app. Real-time console data helped us improve the home screen to make the app easier to understand:

  • bolding important loan details on cards
  • showing clearer error states when data did not load
  • adding actionable guidance during new loan application flows

Setting the tone

With design and content, we rolled out iterative bug-fix updates to signal to customers that we were actively improving the experience, not just patching issues.

04 · Transform

Repayment journey transformation

Before

  • Unclear visibility into upcoming loan dues
  • Customers struggled to identify payable amounts
  • Multiple active loans created servicing confusion
  • Convenience fees added repayment friction
  • Branch dependency remained high for basic servicing

After

  • Upcoming dues surfaced clearly on the home screen
  • Payable amounts became immediately visible
  • Loan cards simplified multi-loan repayment visibility
  • UPI-based payments reduced repayment friction
  • Customers completed servicing directly within the app

Repayments were the most important journey in the customer lifecycle. They affect credit score, future eligibility, and the customer's confidence in the product.

05 · Outcome

The final act: app ratings

Once customers experienced a simpler repayment flow and received timely notification nudges, engagement across dashboards grew rapidly — active users, impressions, and login conversions all saw strong growth.

It was now time to strengthen ratings, because app-store reputation was part of the brand itself. After each successful repayment we introduced a rating prompt at the right moment — not too early, not randomly. That small change significantly improved both the quality and volume of customer reviews.

Within 45 days of launch:

The product was now better positioned for:

  • loan top-ups and cross-sell opportunities
  • real-time repayment communication
  • reduced branch dependency during servicing
  • higher customer trust across servicing journeys

Internally, the work also renewed confidence in the product direction across teams.

Improving trust in financial products rarely comes from one large feature launch — it comes from consistently reducing friction in high-stakes customer moments.
