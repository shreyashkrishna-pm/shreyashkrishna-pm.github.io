The problem

Most users were in tier 2/3 cities with Android driving 86% of usage. The app underperformed at 2.2 / 5 on iOS — unclear loan status, session timeouts, and convenience fees pushed customers back to branches for basic servicing.

The approach

Cleaned up instrumentation across critical flows, simplified the repayment journey with UPI-led payments, and introduced well-timed rating prompts after successful repayments. Shipped iteratively to signal active care.
