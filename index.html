Doubling the Loans App Rating

Outcome

2.2 → 4.4

App Store rating in 45 days. Google Play 3.6 → 4.1.

Redesigned the repayment journey, stabilized the experience for tier 2 & 3 India users, and rebuilt customer trust through rapid iterative releases.

Read the case study →
App Store4.4from 2.2 in 45 days
Google Play4.1Android-first market
Monthly Active Users90K → 166Kpost repayment redesign
Weekly reviews400+improved app-store visibility

The problem

Most users were in tier 2/3 cities with Android driving 86% of usage. The app underperformed at 2.2 / 5 on iOS — unclear loan status, session timeouts, and convenience fees pushed customers back to branches for basic servicing.

The approach

Cleaned up instrumentation across critical flows, simplified the repayment journey with UPI-led payments, and introduced well-timed rating prompts after successful repayments. Shipped iteratively to signal active care.
